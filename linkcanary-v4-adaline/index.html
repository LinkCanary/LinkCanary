Questions, answered

Everything you might ask.

How often does LinkCanary check my links?+
As often as every 30 seconds on paid plans, and every 5 minutes on the free plan. You can set different intervals per monitor — tight checks for your checkout flow, relaxed ones for your blog archive.
What exactly can it monitor?+
Websites and landing pages, API endpoints, internal and outbound links, redirects, SSL certificates, domain expiry, keyword presence, and specific status codes. If it speaks HTTP, the canary can watch it.
How do alerts reach me?+
Email, Slack, Discord, SMS, PagerDuty, Microsoft Teams, or any webhook. Every alert includes the failing URL, status code, response time, region, and a screenshot — and we notify you again the moment it recovers.
Is there really a free plan?+
Yes — 50 checks free, forever, no credit card required. It's built for personal sites and side-projects, and you can upgrade the moment you need tighter intervals or more channels.
Can I monitor staging or password-protected sites?+
Absolutely. Add custom request headers, basic-auth credentials, or bearer tokens per monitor, and LinkCanary will check authenticated and internal environments just like production.
How is this different from Google Search Console?+
Search Console reports problems days later, and only for pages Google has crawled. LinkCanary checks your links on your schedule and alerts you in seconds — covering uptime, APIs, SSL, and outbound links that Search Console never touches.
